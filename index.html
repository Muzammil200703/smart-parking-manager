How It Works

  • Entry: Register a vehicle and the system assigns the first available slot automatically.
  • Full lot: When all slots are occupied, vehicles join a first-in, first-out waiting queue.
  • Exit: Search by registration number — the system calculates the duration and fee, frees the slot, and assigns it to the next vehicle in the queue.
